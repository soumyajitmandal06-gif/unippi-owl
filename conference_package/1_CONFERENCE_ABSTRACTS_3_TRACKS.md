## Track A: Open-Source Bioinformatics & Structural AI Auditing (ISMB/BOSC, 3DSIG, APBJC, RECOMB)
**Title:** UniPPI-OWL v1.8: A Zero-Dependency Dual-Layer Biophysical Engine for Auditing AlphaFold3 Interfaces, Silencing Disordered Hallucinations, and Screening Mutational Landscapes

While AlphaFold3, ESM3, and Boltz-1 have transformed biomolecular structure prediction, their global confidence metrics (ipTM) suffer from two critical failure modes in high-throughput interactome screens: (1) long non-interacting intrinsically disordered regions (IDRs) artificially dilute global ipTM in true complexes, and (2) spurious close-range contacts within low-confidence loops (pLDDT < 40, PAE > 20 A) generate false-positive contact energies. Here, we present UniPPI-OWL v1.8, an open-source, dual-layer biophysical auditing engine implemented identically in a zero-dependency Python 3 standard-library CLI (unippi_cli.py, wrapped in Nextflow DSL2 and Snakemake) and a 100% client-side browser application (index.html). Using 14.0 A 3D spatial voxel hashing (53.8x pruning speedup on 3,000-residue complexes) and 64-point Golden-Spiral Shrake-Rupley SASA integration, UniPPI-OWL couples local PAE coherence (LIS) with confidence-weighted 8-channel solvation thermodynamics (dG_uni, Kd), Setchenow/Debye-Huckel ionic strength (10-1000 mM NaCl), pH titration, phosphorylation/metal gating, and 20-amino-acid saturation mutagenesis (ddG). Across 24 cross-mechanism complexes and 32 experimental mutations (SKEMPI 2.0 and Arabidopsis bZIPs), UniPPI-OWL achieves Pearson r = 0.984 (RMSE = 0.30 kcal/mol on true binders), mutational ddG correlation r = 0.997 (RMSE = 0.22 kcal/mol), and ROC-AUC = 1.000 (MCC = 1.00 at frozen threshold UIS = 0.5000).

## Track B: Protein Engineering, Biophysics & Mutational Landscapes (EMBO, Keystone, Protein Society)
**Title:** Bridging Predicted 3D Coordinates to Physical Binding Free Energy (dG, Kd) and Epistatic Mutational Landscapes (ddG) Without Server Infrastructure

Translating predicted biomolecular coordinates into physical binding free energies (dG, Kd) and site-directed mutational shifts (ddG) remains a central bottleneck in protein engineering. We developed UniPPI-OWL v1.8, a hybrid contact-gated solvation and mutational screening engine that blends confidence-weighted interfacial contact classes with 64-point Shrake-Rupley buried surface area (wBSA), Non-Interacting Surface (NIS) electrostatics, Debye-Huckel ionic screening, Setchenow hydrophobic salting-out, and alpha-helical Proline kink penalties above a +2.10 kcal/mol cratic entropy barrier. Tested on the 6-chain crystallographic asymmetric unit of Barnase-Barstar (PDB: 1BRS), UniPPI-OWL strips crystal waters, isolates pairwise subunit SASA (BSA = 1,546.7 A^2, dG_uni = -12.33 kcal/mol), and recovers all seven experimental energetic hotspots (D:ASP39, A:HIS102, A:ARG83, D:TYR29, A:ARG59, A:ARG87, D:ASP35). Across 24 complexes and 32 SKEMPI 2.0 / coiled-coil mutations, UniPPI-OWL achieves dG Pearson r = 0.984 (RMSE = 0.30 kcal/mol) and ddG Pearson r = 0.997 (Spearman rho = 0.995, RMSE = 0.22 kcal/mol).

## Track C: Plant Molecular Biology & Arabidopsis VIP1 Signaling (ICAR 2027, ASPB, CSHL Plant)
**Title:** Biophysical Modeling of Arabidopsis VIP1 (Group I/E bZIP) Dimerization Selectivity, Osmotic Salt-Stress Stabilization, and CPK21/PP2A-Mediated 14-3-3 Gating

In Arabidopsis thaliana, the osmosensory transcription factor VIP1 (AtbZIP51, Group I) regulates hypo-osmotic and mechanical stress responses through selective leucine-zipper dimerization and phosphorylation-dependent cytoplasmic retention by 14-3-3 (GRF6) proteins. Using UniPPI-OWL v1.8, we resolve four distinct mechanistic classes (Patterns A-D) across the VIP1 signaling network: (1) high-affinity Pattern A dimerization of the VIP1 homodimer (UIS = 0.777, dG_uni = -10.35 kcal/mol, Kd = 25.89 nM) and VIP1-AtbZIP61 heterodimer (dG_uni = -10.10 kcal/mol); (2) Pattern C rejection of the non-interacting AtbZIP34 homodimer (UIS = 0.120, dG_uni = +5.00 kcal/mol) and its epistatic rescue via double-chain Pro->Leu substitution (ddG_1,2 = -5.10 kcal/mol); (3) Setchenow hydrophobic salting-out stabilization of the VIP1 zipper under 800 mM NaCl osmotic stress (-10.35 to -11.89 kcal/mol); and (4) Pattern B rescue of the IDR-diluted VIP1-14-3-3 (GRF6) complex under CPK21 phosphorylation locking (-7.61 to -9.81 kcal/mol), Ser->Asp phospho-mimetic substitution (ddG = -0.58 kcal/mol), and PP2A dephosphorylation release (-4.51 kcal/mol).
