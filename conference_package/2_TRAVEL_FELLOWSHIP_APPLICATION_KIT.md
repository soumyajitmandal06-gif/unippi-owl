## 1. Statement of Scientific Merit & Open-Science Democratization
I am applying for travel fellowship support to present UniPPI-OWL v1.8, an open-source, zero-dependency computational biophysics engine and client-side web resource that bridges 3D structure predictions (AlphaFold3, ESM3, Boltz-1) and experimental crystal structures to physical binding free energies (dG, Kd) and site-directed mutational landscapes (ddG). Validated across 24 cross-mechanism complexes (r = 0.984, ROC-AUC = 1.000, MCC = 1.00) and 32 SKEMPI 2.0 / Arabidopsis bZIP mutations (r = 0.997, RMSE = 0.22 kcal/mol), UniPPI-OWL v1.8 executes 100% locally in browser RAM or standard-library Python 3 with an automated 16-gate regression suite.
